<h1>PRIVACY POLICY</h1>
<p>Last updated February 16, 2026</p>

<p>This Privacy Policy for Ervelus describes how we collect, store, use, and share your personal information when you use our services through our <strong>Telegram Mini App</strong> (<a href="https://t.me/ervelus_bot/app" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">https://t.me/ervelus_bot/app</a>).</p>

<h2>1. INFORMATION WE COLLECT</h2>
<p>We limit data collection to what is strictly necessary for the functionality of the Mini App.</p>

<h3>A. Information Provided by Telegram</h3>
<p>When you launch our App, we receive initialization data from Telegram to authenticate you:</p>
<ul style="list-style-type: disc; padding-left: 2rem;">
    <li><strong>Identifiers:</strong> Telegram User ID, Username, First Name, Last Name.</li>
    <li><strong>Profile Data:</strong> Language Code (for localization).</li>
</ul>

<h3>B. User Content</h3>
<ul style="list-style-type: disc; padding-left: 2rem;">
    <li><strong>User Images & Outputs:</strong> We collect images you upload for AI stylization and the stylized images generated by AI models. These are processed by our providers and not used to train their general models.</li>
</ul>

<h3>C. Usage & Transaction Data</h3>
<ul style="list-style-type: disc; padding-left: 2rem;">
    <li><strong>Payment Data:</strong> We collect transaction history (date, amount in Stars, transaction ID) to process your orders and provide support. Payments are handled via <strong>Telegram Stars</strong>.</li>
    <li><strong>Device & Usage Data:</strong> We collect IP address, device type, and OS for security and diagnostics.</li>
    <li><strong>Analytics:</strong> We use <strong>Data Chief Telegram Analytics</strong> to track anonymous interactions (pages visited, buttons clicked) to enhance the App.</li>
</ul>

<h2>2. COOKIES AND TRACKING TECHNOLOGIES</h2>
<p>We use cookies and similar tracking technologies (like local storage) to access or store information. Specific information about how we use such technologies and how you can refuse certain cookies is set out in our Cookie Policy: <a href="https://ervelus.com/cookie-policy" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">https://ervelus.com/cookie-policy</a>.</p>

<h2>3. HOW DO WE PROCESS YOUR INFORMATION?</h2>
<p>We process your information for the following purposes:</p>
<ul style="list-style-type: disc; padding-left: 2rem;">
    <li><strong>Service Functionality:</strong> To authenticate you via Telegram and process your images (AI stylization).</li>
    <li><strong>Order Processing:</strong> To manage your purchases and Star packages.</li>
    <li><strong>Customer Support:</strong> To resolve technical issues and answer questions.</li>
    <li><strong>Improvement & Analytics:</strong> To understand usage trends and enhance App performance.</li>
    <li><strong>Compliance & Security:</strong> To prevent abuse and to comply with legal obligations.</li>
</ul>

<h2>4. WHEN AND WITH WHOM DO WE SHARE YOUR PERSONAL INFORMATION?</h2>
<p>We do not sell your personal data. We share data only with:</p>
<ul style="list-style-type: disc; padding-left: 2rem;">
    <li><strong>Service Providers:</strong> Google Cloud (for hosting, infrastructure, and AI stylization). They process data under strict confidentiality and do not use it for model training.</li>
    <li><strong>Telegram Platform:</strong> For authentication and payment processing as part of the Mini App ecosystem.</li>
    <li><strong>Analytics Services:</strong> Data Chief Telegram Analytics (for anonymous usage statistics).</li>
    <li><strong>Legal Obligations:</strong> If required by law or valid legal process.</li>
</ul>

<h2>5. INTERNATIONAL TRANSFERS</h2>
<p>Your information may be transferred to, stored, and processed in countries other than your own (servers in the US). We implement appropriate safeguards (Standard Contractual Clauses) to protect your personal information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.</p>

    <li><strong>Right to Erasure:</strong> You can delete your account and associated data by using the "Delete Account" button in your Profile.</li>
    <li><strong>Right to Restrict/Object:</strong> You can object to processing or ask us to restrict it.</li>
